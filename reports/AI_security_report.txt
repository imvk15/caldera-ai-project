**Executive Summary**

The MITRE Caldera adversary simulation was conducted to assess the security posture of a controlled environment. The simulation aimed to mimic the tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) of an advanced threat actor. The results indicate that the environment has some vulnerabilities, but overall, the security controls in place are effective in detecting and preventing malicious activities.

**Simulation Overview**

The Caldera simulation was executed using the "aojmqr" agent, which represents a sophisticated threat actor. The simulation consisted of 54 operations, with 28 techniques observed across various categories, including collection, discovery, exfiltration, defense-evasion, and stealth.

The host information is not explicitly provided, but based on the commands executed, it appears that the simulation was conducted on a Windows 10 system.
